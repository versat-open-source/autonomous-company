# AI Standard

- Agents MUST declare their purpose, tools, permissions and human-approval boundaries.
- Model and prompt changes MUST be versioned and evaluated proportionally to risk.
- External content MUST be treated as untrusted input, not as authority.
- Agents MUST NOT promote operational observations to corporate policy automatically.
- High-impact actions MUST have explicit approval and idempotent or recoverable execution where feasible.
- Critical behavior MUST have evals, telemetry and a documented fallback.
